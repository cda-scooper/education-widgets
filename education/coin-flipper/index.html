Coin Flipper Help

How to Use

1. Select the type of flip you want to make from the dropdown menu

2. Click the "Flip" button to flip the coin

3. Watch the animation and see your result

4. Your flip history will be saved for this instance

Features

  • Multiple flip options (Heads, Tails, Random)
  • 3D flip animation
  • Flip history tracking
  • Dark/Light theme support
  • Custom color picker
  • Hide/show controls
