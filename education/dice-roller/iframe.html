Dice Roller Help

How to Use

1. Select the type of die you want to roll from the dropdown menu

2. Click the "Roll" button to roll the die

3. Watch the animation and see your result

4. Your roll history will be saved for this instance

Features

  • Multiple die types (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d100)
  • Animated roll effect
  • Roll history tracking
  • Dark/Light theme support
  • Custom color picker
  • Hide/show controls
