How to Use

Track student reading time with a fun reward system!

  • Enter student name and click "Start Reading" to begin tracking
  • Click "Stop Reading" when the student finishes
  • Students receive a random reward when they complete their reading
  • Export the reading data to CSV for record keeping
  • 🌓 Toggle between light and dark theme
  • 🎨 Change the widget's color
  • ⚙️ Adjust settings
  • 👁️ Show/hide the widget
