Noise Meter Help

How to Use

1. Click "Start Monitoring" to begin measuring noise levels

2. The thermometer will show current noise level:

  • Green: Quiet (Good for learning)
  • Yellow: Moderate (Getting loud)
  • Red: Loud (Too noisy)

3. Click "Stop Monitoring" to pause the meter

4. Your noise history will be saved for this instance

Features

  • Real-time noise level monitoring
  • Visual thermometer display
  • Noise history tracking
  • Dark/Light theme support
  • Custom color picker
  • Hide/show controls
